How to play: both detectives get a secret person. On your turn, type any yes/no question about your opponent's person — facial features, identity, vibes, whatever. Your opponent studies their picture and answers. Then you eliminate every person who doesn't match, tapping cards to inspect them up close. When only one candidate remains, guess their name — a wrong guess is an instant loss.

Identity is on the badge: cards show pronouns (she/her, he/him, they/them). Asking "is your person a woman?" is about identity; "is your person female?" is about appearance. They are not the same thing. In Woke Mode they are periodically not the same thing. Tread carefully.

Tough packs (Sweden, Cameroon, Saudi Arabia, South India, Lookalikes) make everyone look alike — dig into earlobes, eyebrow thickness and nose bridges. Win matches to earn 🪙 and unlock packs.
